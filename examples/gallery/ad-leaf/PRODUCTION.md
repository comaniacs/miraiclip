# LEAF: "One page thin"

A 30-second ad for an invented e-reader, **LEAF**. It is HTML and CSS 3D only, with no WebGL, filmed in one camera take on one deterministic timeline.

## A. Treatment

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | LEAF, a thin e-reader with a paper-like screen and a warm reading light. The brand and product are invented, and no performance figures are claimed. |
| **Audience** | Readers aged 25–55 who travel, commute, or read in bed and own too many books to carry. |
| **Message** | *Your whole library, one page thin.* |
| **Differentiator** | It feels like paper: a page that holds every page. |
| **Emotion** | Wonder (a page drifting in the dark), recognition (all your books), calm (warm light, one quiet slab). |
| **Hook** | A single printed page falls toward the lens through a pool of light. |
| **Device** | **The page.** One page becomes many; many compress into the screen; the screen turns its own page; the pages orbit as your library; they fold back into one device. |
| **Art direction** | Ink-blue night `#0b1020`, warm paper `#f4efe4`, a graphite device body with a soft-touch finish, an amber reading light `#ffb867`, and one accent, leaf green `#7fd49b`. |
| **Camera** | Tracks the falling page, pulls back through the swirl, pushes into the screen (macro), orbits the device inside the ring of pages, and settles on a hero three-quarter view. |
| **Sound** | Original synth. Paper flutters, a soft felt-piano motif, a "compress" whoosh into a soft thud, a page-turn swish, a warm pad swell, and a final chord. |
| **CTA** | **LEAF** · *Your whole library, one page thin.* · "Pre-order now". |

## B. Script

| Time | Scene | Action | Camera | Copy | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.0 | Hook | A page reading "Chapter One" flutters down through a pool of light, turning as it falls. Other pages appear out of the dark. | Tracking the page, close. | "Every story you love…" | Paper flutter; one felt-piano note. |
| 4.0–9.0 | Introduction | Forty pages swirl into a vortex around a centre, then fall into a stack that compresses into a thin graphite slab. The device is born and its screen shows the page. | Pull back and around the swirl, then frame the device. | "…in one page." · **LEAF** | Whoosh into a soft thud on the compression; the motif begins. |
| 9.0–15.0 | Discovery | Macro on the screen: crisp text. The reading light slides from cool to warm amber as a slider fills. | Push in to the screen, then drift across it. | "Reads like paper." · "Warm light for late chapters." | A soft tick on the slider; the pad warms. |
| 15.0–21.0 | Experience | A page turns on the screen, peeling off the device as a real page, and the pages fly out into an orbiting ring: your library around you. | An orbit through the ring. | "Every book you own. Everywhere you go." | Page-turn swish; the motif builds. |
| 21.0–26.0 | Climax | The ring tightens and every page dives back into the screen in a spiral. The screen flashes, then shows the LEAF cover. | Pull up, then settle. | — | Rising swell; a chord on the flash. |
| 26.0–30.0 | CTA | The hero device stands at an angle in a pool of light; the lockup is on the left. Held from 28.6 s. | Settled. | **LEAF** · "Your whole library, one page thin." · "Pre-order now" | Final chord; it ends at 30 s. |
