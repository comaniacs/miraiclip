Course scenes and a `course` format. Seven `scene-course-*` blocks (`COURSE_SCENES`) cover a course title, a syllabus, a lesson in a player, projects, an instructor, skills with a certificate, and an enrol closer. They also join `FILM_SCENES`. `FORMAT_INFO.course` gives the learner-led beat order. The new `academy` reference style uses warm white, navy, a serif and a notebook grid. The typed hook now sizes its type to its longest line (`len` field), so long questions stay in frame.
