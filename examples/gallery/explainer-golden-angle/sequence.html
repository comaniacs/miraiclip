This flower is doing math.
Every new seed turns by the same angle, again and again.
One rule.
Turn by θ. Nudge outward. Repeat a thousand times.
But which angle?
¼ turn → 4 spokes. Wasted gaps.
⅓ turn → 3 spokes.
⅖ turn → 5 spokes.
Any neat fraction eventually lines up into spokes. Even 137° leaves gaps.
The golden angle.
360° × (1 − 1/φ) ≈ 137.5°
φ ≈ 1.618 is the number fractions approximate worst, so no two seeds ever line up. Perfect packing.
