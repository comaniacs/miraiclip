# TILDE: "Nothing to hide"

A 30-second ad for an invented drink, **TILDE Sparkling Citrus**. It is HTML and CSS 3D only, with no WebGL, filmed in one camera take on one deterministic timeline.

## A. Treatment

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | TILDE, a sparkling water made from three ingredients: spring water, lemon and lime. No sugar, no sweeteners. (Invented brand, invented product.) |
| **Audience** | 20–40, label-readers who want something bright that isn't a soda. |
| **Message** | *Nothing to hide.* The ingredient list is short enough to show you whole. |
| **Differentiator** | Radical transparency: the ad literally peels the label off the can to show what's in it. |
| **Emotion** | Curiosity (one bubble in the dark), then refreshment, delight, and trust. |
| **Hook** | Macro on a single bubble rising through dark water. It pops, and the pop lights the world. |
| **Device** | **The label.** It wraps the can, peels flat to become the ingredient card, and wraps back on. The tilde wave (~) is the brand line that draws the logo. |
| **Art direction** | Deep teal night `#05222a`, brushed aluminium, a sunny citrus label `#ffd23f` with coral `#ff6b4a` and cream type, glassy bubbles, translucent lemon and lime slices. |
| **Camera** | Rises with the bubble; pulls back down the can; frontal for the unwrap; a low orbit through the citrus; up for the climax; settles on a hero three-quarter view. |
| **Sound** | Original synth: an underwater hush, a bright pop that starts a light, plucky groove (112 BPM), paper peel and zip sounds, citrus "plinks", a fizz swell into the climax, and a clean final chord. |
| **CTA** | TILDE · *Nothing to hide. Everything to taste.* · "Find it chilled". |

## B. Script

| Time | Scene | Action | Camera | Copy | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.0 | Hook | One large bubble rises through dark teal with a few small ones. At 3.6 s it reaches the can's lid and pops: a ring flash, and the key light comes on. | Macro, rising with the bubble (dist 520). | none | Underwater hush, a soft rising tone, the **pop**. |
| 4.0–9.0 | Introduction | The pop's light sweeps down the can. It turns a little and the label reads TILDE. | Pull back and tilt down to a 3/4 view; the can moves right. | "Sparkling citrus water." / "TILDE" | The groove starts on the pop. |
| 9.0–15.0 | Discovery | The label peels off the can strip by strip, straightens into a flat card, and slides left. Three ingredients count in beside it, then "0 g sugar". | Frontal and wide, holding the card and the bare can. | "Nothing to hide." · `3 ingredients` · `Spring water · Lemon · Lime` · `0 g sugar · 0 sweeteners` | Paper peel; a tick for each line. |
| 15.0–21.0 | Experience | The label wraps back on. Lemon and lime slices drop in and orbit, bubbles stream up past the camera, and the can shines. | A low orbit around the can (yaw 0° → 120°). | "Bright. Crisp. Honest." | The groove builds; citrus plinks. |
| 21.0–26.0 | Climax | A ring of bubbles bursts upward and a coral tilde wave sweeps around the can, drawing the logo. | Rising to a hero angle. | — | Fizz swell, then a chord hit. |
| 26.0–30.0 | CTA | The hero can is on the right with a contact shadow, and the lockup is on the left. Held from 28.6 s. | Settled. | **TILDE** · "Nothing to hide. Everything to taste." · "Find it chilled" | Final chord; it ends at 30 s. |
