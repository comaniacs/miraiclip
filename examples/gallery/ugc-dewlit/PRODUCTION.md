# DEWLIT Cloud Milk Serum: UGC ad (everything but the human)

A 30-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) UGC-style ad for an **invented** skincare brand. Everything except the presenter is built and rendered:
- the product inserts (a 3D-styled bottle and a macro of the texture)
- captions, synced to the script
- the brand end card
- the music and sound design

The presenter is six clearly marked **placeholder clips** on their own track, at the scripted timings. Replace each with real phone footage (or an AI avatar, disclosed as such) and render again.

## B. Presenter (casting and performance spec)

- **Who:** "Mira", 27–32, a warm medium skin tone with visible natural texture (no heavy smoothing), dark shoulder-length hair loosely clipped back, a few strands loose.
- **Wardrobe:** an oatmeal ribbed knit and small gold hoops. Light makeup: groomed brows, tinted lip balm, no foundation-heavy look.
- **Personality:** calm and wry; smiles with her eyes; talks to the lens like texting a friend.
- **Voice:** a natural mid-pace delivery (about 2.6 words per second), small pauses, rising "right?" energy only once. Neutral North-American or British accent to match the target market.
- **Behaviour:** natural blinks, glances down at the product when handling it, looks back to the lens to land each line. Hands relaxed: holds the bottle low in frame with a thumb on the label edge, never hiding it.
