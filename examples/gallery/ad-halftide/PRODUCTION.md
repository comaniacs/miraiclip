# HALFTIDE: "Colour that moves like water"

A 30-second ad for an invented fountain-pen ink, **HALFTIDE**. It is HTML, SVG and CSS 3D only, with no WebGL, filmed in one camera take on one deterministic timeline. The brief asked for **fluid motion**: the ink is real metaball "goo" (blobs that merge and split like liquid), and every move flows on continuous curves, so nothing starts or stops on a hard edge.

## A. Treatment

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | HALFTIDE, a range of fountain-pen inks; the hero colour is Tidewater Blue. The brand is invented, and no performance claims are made. |
| **Audience** | People who write by hand: journal keepers, letter writers, artists, pen collectors. |
| **Message** | *Colour that moves like water.* The ink flows, shades and blooms. |
| **Emotion** | Fascination (a drop blooming on wet paper), then pleasure, then desire. |
| **Hook** | Macro: one drop of ink falls and blooms across wet paper. |
| **Device** | **The ink itself.** One continuous body of liquid blooms, spirals into a pool that raises the bottle, writes its own line, splits into three colours, and pours back to fill the bottle. |
| **Art direction** | Warm cotton paper `#f3eee4` on a dark walnut desk `#1a120d`. Inks: Tidewater Blue `#1f4f9e`, Kelp `#1d7a6b`, Amber `#d4892a`. Glass, crisp black type. |
| **Camera** | Follows the drop down, rises over the bloom, glides to the bottle, drifts over the page as it writes, circles with the colour ribbons, and settles on a hero three-quarter view. |
| **Sound** | Original synth. A drip, a soft bloom swell, a flowing harp-like arpeggio, nib scratches, liquid swirls, a pour that fills the bottle, and a final chord. |
| **CTA** | **HALFTIDE** · *Colour that moves like water.* · "Explore the 12 tides" (the range is invented). |

## B. Script

| Time | Scene | Action | Camera | Copy | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.0 | Hook | A drop falls (0–1.5 s), hits the wet paper, and blooms into tendrils that curl outward. | Following the drop down, then rising over the bloom. | — | A drip, then the bloom swell. |
| 4.0–9.0 | Introduction | The bloom swirls into a vortex that drains toward a point, and from the pool a glass bottle rises full of blue ink (6.6–8.4 s). | A glide to the bottle. | "Every drop," / "a tide." · HALFTIDE | A swirl into a soft glass "clink". |
| 9.0–15.0 | Discovery | Ink flows from the bottle's base across the page and writes a looping line, the head of the stroke a moving blob of liquid. | Drifting over the page. | "Write like water." · "Shades as it dries." | Nib scratch; the arpeggio begins. |
| 15.0–21.0 | Experience | Three colours (blue, kelp, amber) flow as ribbons around the bottle, merging where they meet. | Circling with the ribbons. | "Twelve colours." / "One flow." | The arpeggio builds; liquid swirls. |
| 21.0–26.0 | Climax | Every ribbon spirals into the bottle's base, and the ink level rises to full with a ripple. | Rising to the hero angle. | — | The pour, and a chord as it fills. |
| 26.0–30.0 | CTA | The hero bottle sits on the inked page; the lockup is on the left. Held from 28.6 s. | Settled. | **HALFTIDE** · "Colour that moves like water." · "Explore the 12 tides" | Final chord; it ends at 30 s. |
