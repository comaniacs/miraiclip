# Scorecard (review)

`check_sequence` measures carry, framing and rhythm. These points it can't measure. Grade every draft against them, say what you changed, and show the contact sheet.

1. **A hook in the first second.** Something moves and raises a question before any text is read. Fail: a logo, a title card, a fade in from black.
2. **One subject the camera chases.** A single element the viewer can name (the dot, the cursor, the vase) is followed through the film. Fail: the camera wanders between equal elements.
3. **A rest before the payoff.** One declared `tl.rest` (0.6–1.2 s) before the key moment, so the payoff lands. Fail: constant motion with no pause, or an undeclared still.
4. **Small elements on a big ground.** The subject is small against a large ground at some point, so scale changes can be felt. Fail: everything fills the frame at all times.
5. **No confetti or flourishes that aren't the product's own behaviour.** A particle burst, lens flare or sparkle that the product wouldn't do on its own is removed. A button squashing when pressed is fine.
6. **A lockup the cast folds into.** The last beat is the logo or name made out of what has been on screen, not a new card. Fail: a fresh end card fades in.
7. **A look chosen fresh.** Palette, type and ground are chosen for this subject (see the `look` skill), not reused from the last film or the sample.

Grade each: pass, weak (say how to fix) or fail (fix now). Don't present a draft with a fail.
