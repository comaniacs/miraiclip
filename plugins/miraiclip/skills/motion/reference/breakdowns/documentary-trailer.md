# Breakdown: documentary trailer (40 s)

A grammar study of a style, not a description of one particular film: the trailer for a documentary or a true story. Still photographs carry it, kept alive by slow pushes and parallax. Titles rise out of darkness, facts are stated plainly, and the music builds to one reveal. Its pace is slower than motion graphics, but it is never still.

Use it to learn the grammar. Build your own film's version of each idea; don't copy the beats.

## Look

- **Background:** near-black, with fine grain and a slight vignette. Letterbox bars are optional, and when used text stays inside the middle 70%.
- **Type:** a restrained serif or a light condensed sans, generously tracked for titles (caps, `letter-spacing: .3em`) and plain for facts. Off-white, never pure white.
- **Colour:** desaturated pictures (or duotone into one warm accent). The accent appears once or twice: a date, the title.
- **Pictures:** real archive stills, documents and places. Each one gets a slow move. Captions name the place and date in small caps.
- **Words on screen:** one short line per card, sometimes a single date or number.

## The grammar, in short

- **Stills are never still.** Every picture has a slow push, a pan or parallax for its whole time on screen. The camera move carries across the cut (a blur-through on a continuing push).
- **Plain words.** Facts in short declarative lines. Only real quotes, with the speaker. Dates and places as small-caps captions.
- **Acceleration.** Shot lengths shrink as the film builds (4 s → 2 s → 1 s → 0.5 s), then stop dead for the reveal. The rhythm tells the story.
- **Darkness is the transition.** Titles come out of black, and pictures fade up from black. Use dips only at the act breaks, not between every still.
- **One number.** A single counted figure is a turning point. A wall of statistics is a report.
- **The accent is saved** for the title and the payoff.

## Timing targets

This is a documentary-paced film, so its targets are lower than motion graphics:

- Motion density at least 0.6 (slow pushes count as motion; they should be visible, not imperceptible).
- Holds over 0.8 s only where meant: the opening caption, the title, the silence.
