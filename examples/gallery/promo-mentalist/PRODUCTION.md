# THE MENTALIST: "Every detail matters" (fan-made promo)

A 30-second **unofficial, fan-made** promotional film for *The Mentalist*, the CBS crime drama that ran from 2008 to 2015 for seven seasons. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by CBS or Warner Bros. Television, and it says so on screen. It uses no official logos, artwork or actor likenesses. The title is set in our own typography, and the only fact stated is the seven seasons. It is HTML and CSS 3D only, with no WebGL, filmed in one camera take.

## A. Treatment

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | *The Mentalist*, the complete series: a consultant who reads people the way others read books. |
| **Audience** | Crime-drama fans, binge-watchers, and anyone who loves a clever reveal. |
| **Message** | *Every detail matters.* Watch closely, because he does. |
| **Device** | **The anamorphic reveal.** Clues float at different depths in a dark mind-space and look random. Seen from exactly the right angle, they line up into a red smiley face: the series' signature warning. The ad makes the viewer do what the show does, look until the pattern appears. |
| **Hook** | A cup of tea in a single spotlight. The surface trembles. "Look closer." The camera dives through the tea into the mind. |
| **Emotion** | Calm curiosity, then the pleasure of deduction, then a chill at the pattern, then the urge to watch. |
| **Art direction** | Noir: near-black `#07080b`, warm tungsten `#ffcf8a`, cream polaroids and index cards, and a single red `#c8102e` for the string and the pattern. |
| **Camera** | A slow push to the cup, a top-down dive through the tea, drifting among the clues, push-ins on three deductions, tracking along the red string, and one decisive swing to the exact viewpoint where the pattern resolves. |
| **Sound** | Original synth. A clock tick, a spoon on porcelain, a low cello-like drone, a pizzicato motif that quickens with the deductions, a string "zip", then silence and a deep hit on the reveal, and a final chord. |
| **CTA** | THE MENTALIST · "All seven seasons. Every detail matters." · "Stream the complete series" · a fan-made disclaimer. |
