# Treatment

Fill this in before building. Keep it to one page: it is a decision, not an essay.

```
Film:        <subject> — <format>, <length> s, <aspect>
Promise:     <what the viewer knows or feels at the end, one line>
World:       <the metaphor the film lives in, one sentence>
Protagonist: <element> → <becomes> → <becomes> → <ends as>
Look:        <palette (bg / ink / accent / second)>, <type>, <finish>, <grid>, <picture treatment>
Camera:      <its character: floating / pushing / drifting …>
Music:       <mood>, <tempo>, hits at <times>; SFX on <moves>

Act 1 — <name> (<start>–<end> s)
| Time | Beat | On screen | Technique | Carries into the next |
…
Act 2 — <name> (<start>–<end> s)
…
End card: <the line>, held <≤1.5> s, still drifting
```

## Worked example

A fictional neighbourhood bakery app, "Crumb", which lets you order bread for collection before it sells out.

```
Film:        Crumb — launch, 15 s, 16:9
Promise:     Order the loaf tonight; it's waiting for you at 8.
World:       The bakery's morning, told by one loaf of bread.
Protagonist: a loaf (a rounded shape) → it rises → it becomes the app's order card →
             the card's "Ready 8:00" becomes a clock → the clock face becomes the logo's O
Look:        flour-white bg #f6f1e7, crust-brown ink #3b2a1f, accent oven orange #e8772e,
             second rye #8a6a4f; a rounded serif for titles, a soft sans for labels;
             paper grain; centred axis; no photos — the loaf is drawn
Camera:      warm and slow: gentle pushes, one tilt when the card appears, a pull back at the end
Music:       light acoustic pluck, ~110 bpm (a bar ≈ 2.2 s), a soft hit at 7.6 s (the order
             is placed); SFX: a pop on the card, ticks on the clock, a bell on the logo

Act 1 — Sold out (0–7.6 s)
| 0.0–1.6 | "By 9, it's gone." | Empty shelf line; the loaf shape slides off it and away | staggered-entrance, motion-blur | The shelf line |
| 1.6–3.2 | "Every morning." | The shelf line becomes a sunrise arc; the loaf rises over it like the sun | morph, path-follow | The loaf |
| 3.2–5.4 | "Not anymore." | The loaf drops into the frame centre and puffs (it rises) | overshoot | The loaf's outline |
| 5.4–7.6 | (no words) | The outline stretches into a rounded card; "Sourdough · 1" types in | morph, typewriter | The card |

Act 2 — Ready at 8 (7.6–15 s)
| 7.6–9.4 | "Order tonight." | A "Reserve" button pops; tap ripple; the card tilts towards us | pop-in, depth-stack | The card's time row |
| 9.4–11.4 | "Ready at 8:00." | "8:00" swells; its digits roll from 23:14 to 8:00 | count-up, zoom-through | The clock digits |
| 11.4–13.5 | "Crumb." | The 0 of 8:00 is pulled out; it becomes the O in the wordmark | carry-over, match-cut | The wordmark |
| 13.5–15.0 | "crumb.app" | The URL rises under the wordmark; slow pull back, grain | mask-reveal, holds | — |
```

Every beat has one idea and one move, and something that carries over. The words are at most 4 per beat. The length is 15 s, as asked.
